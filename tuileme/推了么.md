# 推了么 (Tuileme)
### The AI-Powered Reply Assistant for X / Twitter
### X / Twitter 智能回复助手

---

## /// SYSTEM_OVERVIEW [系统概览]

**推了么** 是一款运行在浏览器上的 X (Twitter) 辅助扩展。它将大语言模型（LLM）的能力无缝注入到你的社交时间线中，让你能以不同的人格、语气瞬间生成高质量回复。

**Tuileme** is a browser extension that supercharges your X (Twitter) experience. It seamlessly injects Large Language Model (LLM) capabilities into your timeline, allowing you to instantly generate high-quality replies with various personas and tones.

> "Don't just reply. Reply with style."
> “不仅是回复，更是风格。”

---

## /// CORE_FEATURES [核心特性]

### 01. Contextual Intelligence
### 语境感知智能

不需要复制粘贴。推了么自动读取当前推文内容，理解上下文，生成切题、有梗、高情商的回复。

No copy-pasting required. Tuileme automatically reads the current tweet, understands the context, and generates relevant, witty, and high-EQ replies.

### 02. Multi-Persona System
### 多重人格系统

内置多种预设人格，支持自定义 Prompt。
- **SHINJI**: 内向、真诚、谨慎
- **ASUKA**: 自信、傲娇、直接
- **REI**: 冷静、分析、简洁
- **YOUR_OWN**: 你完全可以定义属于自己的独特语气

Built-in personas with support for custom prompts.
- **SHINJI**: Reserved, sincere, cautious
- **ASUKA**: Confident, tsundere, direct
- **REI**: Calm, analytical, concise
- **YOUR_OWN**: Define your own unique tone completely

### 03. MAGI UI Interface
### MAGI 风格界面

致敬经典的 EVA MAGI 系统界面。复古未来主义设计，实时流式输出动画，甚至连思考过程（Chain of Thought）都一览无余。

A tribute to the classic EVA MAGI system interface. Retro-futuristic design, real-time streaming output, and visible Chain of Thought process.

### 04. Seamless Integration
### 无缝集成

按钮直接嵌入 X 网页端操作栏。生成满意后，自动填入回复框。如果浏览器限制，自动复制到剪贴板。

Buttons are embedded directly into the X web action bar. Once generated, it auto-fills the reply box. If restricted by the browser, it auto-copies to your clipboard.

## /// HOW_IT_WORKS [工作原理]

1. **HOVER**: Mouse over the **推了么** button on any tweet.
   **悬停**：鼠标悬停在任意推文的 **推了么** 按钮上。

2. **SELECT**: Choose a persona from the popup menu.
   **选择**：从弹出菜单中选择一个人格。

3. **GENERATE**: Click to start. Watch the AI think and write in real-time.
   **生成**：点击开始。实时观看 AI 思考与写作。

## /// PRIVACY_PROTOCOL [隐私协议]

**Your Data Stays Yours.**
**你的数据属于你。**

- **Local Storage**: Your API Key and settings are stored ONLY in your local browser storage.
- **No Server**: We do not have a backend server. We do not collect your data.
- **Direct Connection**: The extension communicates directly between your browser and the OpenAI API (or your custom endpoint).

- **本地存储**：你的 API Key 和设置仅存储在你的本地浏览器中。
- **无服务器**：我们没有后端服务器，不收集你的任何数据。
- **直连通信**：扩展直接在你的浏览器和 OpenAI API（或你的自定义端点）之间通信。
